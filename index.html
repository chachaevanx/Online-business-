🛡️ Protect Yourself When Searching for Online Work

  • Check the employer's official website and job description.
  • Never share your email password, M-Pesa PIN or one-time verification codes.
  • Be cautious of jobs promising guaranteed income or demanding unexplained fees.
  • Check whether the job accepts applicants living in Kenya.
  • Never treat example job titles on this page as proof of an actual vacancy.
